Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2026.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email through Clerk, our authentication provider.

Payment information is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details ourselves.

While the app is running, Guided detects which software application is currently active on your screen so it can teach the right tool. This is processed locally on your device, and the application name is sent to the Anthropic Claude API as part of your request context.

Chat messages you send through the app are transmitted to the Claude API to generate responses.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not use:

  • Advertising trackers
  • Facebook Pixel
  • Google Analytics
  • Browsing history

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the Guided service
  • Process subscription payments
  • Send transactional, account-related emails

Third-party services

Guided relies on the following third-party services:

  • Clerk — authentication
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI responses

Data storage

Your chat history and project data are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing support@guided.build.

Questions about this policy? Email support@guided.build.
