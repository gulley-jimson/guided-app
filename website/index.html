How it works

Three things, working together.

Most tutorials weren't made for your project. Guided learns yours, watches you work, and turns the path into something you can actually finish.

Vision Session

A short, warm intake conversation. Guided gets to know you, your project, and your goals before teaching anything.

Screen Awareness

When you ask "what do I click?", Guided actually looks. It sees what's on your screen and points to the answer.

Personal Roadmap

A custom learning path built from the conversation, tailored to your skill level and timeline. Real milestones, real progress.

Works with

Every tool you're already using.

Guided is software-agnostic. Whatever app is in front of you, it adapts.
